Background

About

I integrate computational chemistry, machine learning, and experimental research to accelerate materials discovery and advance technologies for clean energy, critical-material recovery, and industrial decarbonization. I am a Postdoctoral Associate with the Global Research Initiative for Sustainable Low-Carbon Unconventional Resources in the Department of Chemical and Petroleum Engineering at the University of Calgary.

My research focuses on heterogeneous catalysis and electrocatalysis. Using density functional theory (DFT), high-throughput screening, machine learning, and experimental approaches, I investigate catalytic mechanisms and structure–activity relationships to guide rational catalyst design. My work spans CO2 electroreduction, Fischer–Tropsch synthesis for synthetic fuel production, and hydrogen generation.

My goal is to translate fundamental chemical insights into practical innovations that accelerate catalyst discovery, enable cleaner fuel production, and support sustainable resource use and low-carbon technologies.

My research has appeared in Nature Communications, ACS Catalysis, Angewandte Chemie, Advanced Materials, Nanoscale Advances.

Research Themes

Research

My work uses first-principles simulation and machine learning to understand catalytic mechanisms at the atomic scale and translate that understanding into design principles for clean energy catalysts.

⚗️

CO2 Electroreduction

Applying DFT and ML screening to identify selective transition metal and single-atom electrocatalysts for CO2-to-multicarbon product conversion. Mechanistic studies of C–C coupling and selectivity descriptors.

⚡

Fischer–Tropsch & PtL Fuels

Experimental investigation of Fischer–Tropsch synthesis including catalyst synthesis, characterization (XRD, BET, TPR), reactor testing, and activity and selectivity measurements — targeting jet-range hydrocarbon production from syngas and CO₂.

🤖

Machine Learning for Catalysis

High-throughput DFT combined with random forest, gradient boosting, and active learning frameworks to screen large compositional spaces and identify structure–activity relationships for clean energy catalysts.

✈️

Sustainable Aviation Fuel

Systematic computational review of RWGS, FTS, and methanol-to-jet catalytic pathways for e-SAF production. Perspective review covering DFT, ML, and MLIP approaches to accelerate SAF catalyst discovery.

🔬

Nanocatalyst Modelling

First-principles DFT investigation of Pt cluster structures, oxidation states, and elementary catalytic steps on oxide supports. Automated high-throughput calculation workflows for systematic cluster screening.

💎

Critical Materials & Li Extraction

Computational and experimental research on selective lithium extraction from brines, developing materials and process insights relevant to battery supply chain decarbonization.
