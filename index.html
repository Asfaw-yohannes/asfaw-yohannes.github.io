Background

About

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Chemical and Petroleum Engineering at the University of Calgary, where I apply electronic structure methods, high-throughput screening, and machine learning to accelerate catalyst discovery for clean energy applications.

My research spans CO₂ electroreduction, Fischer–Tropsch synthesis, Power-to-Liquid (PtL) fuel production, and hydrogen production — connecting atomistic mechanistic understanding with the design principles needed to move these technologies toward commercial scale.

I hold a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany, where I studied Pt nanocatalysts for energy conversion using density functional theory. Before my doctoral research I was a Lecturer and Assistant Professor in Chemistry at Bahir Dar University, Ethiopia.

I am currently completing a CCUS Advanced Engineering Certificate and an Environmental Management Certificate at the University of Calgary, deepening my expertise in carbon capture and sustainable energy systems.

Computational heterogeneous catalysis — DFT, microkinetic modelling, free energy analysis for CO₂ conversion and Fischer–Tropsch synthesis

Research Themes

Research

My work uses first-principles simulation and machine learning to understand catalytic mechanisms at the atomic scale and translate that understanding into design principles for clean energy catalysts.

⚗️

CO₂ Electroreduction

Applying DFT and ML screening to identify selective transition metal and single-atom electrocatalysts for CO₂-to-multicarbon product conversion. Mechanistic studies of C–C coupling and selectivity descriptors.

⚡

Fischer–Tropsch & PtL Fuels

DFT mechanistic studies of CO activation, chain growth, and selectivity on cobalt and iron carbide surfaces. ML-driven catalyst screening for jet-selective Fischer–Tropsch synthesis from CO₂-derived syngas.

🤖

Machine Learning for Catalysis

High-throughput DFT combined with random forest, gradient boosting, and active learning frameworks to screen large compositional spaces and identify structure–activity relationships for clean energy catalysts.

✈️

Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)

Systematic computational review of RWGS, FTS, and methanol-to-jet catalytic pathways for e-SAF production. Perspective review covering DFT, ML, and MLIP approaches to accelerate SAF catalyst discovery.

🔬

Nanocatalyst Modelling

First-principles DFT investigation of Pt cluster structures, oxidation states, and elementary catalytic steps on oxide supports. Automated high-throughput calculation workflows for systematic cluster screening.

💎

Critical Materials & Li Extraction

Computational and experimental research on selective lithium extraction from brines, developing materials and process insights relevant to battery supply chain decarbonisation.
